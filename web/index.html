DevBot

A small chatbot that answers questions about me and my work — and says "I don't know" instead of making things up. Every reply shows how it was chosen.

Questions it couldn't answer

The Python version writes these to unknown_questions.txt so they can be taught next. Here they stay in your browser only.

    How it works

    The same logic as chatbot.py, reading the same intents.json.

    1. Intents — each topic has example phrases ("patterns") and replies.
    2. Vectorise — TF-IDF turns every pattern, and your message, into numbers.
    3. Match — cosine similarity finds the closest pattern; its topic's reply is used.
    4. Be honest — below 0.3 similarity, DevBot says it doesn't know and logs the question.
