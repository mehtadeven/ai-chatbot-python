    How I work

    1. 📚IntentsEach topic has example phrases ("patterns") and replies in intents.json.
    2. 🔢VectoriseTF-IDF turns every pattern, and your message, into numbers.
    3. 🎯MatchCosine similarity finds the closest pattern, and I use that topic's reply.
    4. 🙊Be honestBelow 0.3 similarity I say I don't know, and note the question.
